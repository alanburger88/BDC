Brand Overview: BDC (Business Development Bank of Canada)
1. Color Palette
Primary Dark / Slate Navy: #182C3D (dominant background for dark sections, footer, and deep contrast panels)

Primary Brand Red: #E01A22 (primary call-to-action buttons, key headline accents, and brand asterisk)

Deep Red / Shadow Red: #BD1219 (hover states, secondary facets of the red graphic element)

Pure White: #FFFFFF (light section backgrounds, pill buttons, header background, dark-mode text)

Off-White / Cool Gray: #F4F5F7 (subtle section cards, secondary card backgrounds)

Body Text (Charcoal): #222222 to #333333 (reading text on light backgrounds)

Muted Gray: #5B6770 (secondary labels, metadata, captions, input borders)

Accent Electric Blue: #2B59FF (featured tool cards, illustration accents)

2. Typography
Font Family: Clean geometric humanist sans-serif (e.g., Helvetica Neue, Arial, or modern geometric grotesques like Inter / Graphik).

Headings (H1/H2):

Weight: 500 (Medium) to 700 (Bold).

Scale: Large, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em), compact line height (1.1 to 1.2).

Features: Sentence case, occasional inline color highlights (e.g., contrasting red words in a black title).

Subheadings / Lead Text:

Weight: 400 (Regular) to 500 (Medium).

Size: 18px – 22px, generous line height (1.4 – 1.5).

Body Copy:

Weight: 400 (Regular).

Size: 15px – 16px, line height 1.5.

Overlines / Category Tags:

Weight: 500 (Medium) or 600 (Semi-bold).

Size: 12px – 13px, subtle letter-spacing.

3. UI Components & Design System
Buttons & CTAs:

Primary Action: Solid red background (#E01A22), white text, full pill shape (border-radius: 9999px), padding approx. 12px 24px.

Secondary / Ghost Action: Transparent background with a crisp 1px–1.5px solid border (white border on dark, slate border on light), matching pill shape.

Text Links: Clean inline link with a trailing red right-arrow icon (→ Learn more).

Cards & Containers:

Generous corner radiuses (border-radius: 12px to 16px).

Overflow hidden, clean padding, subtle border or soft drop shadows (0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)).

Imagery & Motifs:

People-Centric Photography: High-contrast, authentic, warm portraits and real workplace environments focused on diverse entrepreneurs.

Brand Icon: The multi-pointed geometric red/faceted star or asterisk motif used as an illustrative watermark or anchor graphic.

4. Tone & Voice
Tone: Empowering, institutional yet approachable, confident, and direct.

Core Positioning: "The bank for Canadian entrepreneurs."

Copy Style: Action-driven verbs ("Kick your next projects into gear", "Face your next challenges with confidence"), concise value propositions, and entrepreneur-first messaging.
